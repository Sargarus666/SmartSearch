# SmartScan: price policy

## Selection criteria
- Price range for the default shortlist: **15,000–20,000 RUB**.
- Physical RAM: **at least 8 GB**. Virtual RAM expansion does not count.
- New devices only; warranty should be checked with the seller before purchase.
- Catalog models include the exact RAM and storage variant.

## Approximate prices
The website intentionally does **not** scrape retailer prices or claim real-time availability. Each phone has an estimated Russian-market price range stored in `data/phones.json`.

These ranges are for comparing models only. They are not live offers, do not prove local stock in Naberezhnye Chelny, and do not guarantee a particular seller's warranty or delivery terms.

## Store links
A phone's detail page contains links to search for that exact model in DNS, Ситилинк, Ozon, МегаФон, Билайн and МТС. The links are convenience shortcuts; users should confirm the exact RAM/storage variant, price, condition, warranty and availability on the retailer's website.

## Site behavior
Clicking a catalog card opens an internal phone detail page with the product image, specifications, estimated price and store links. Search, filters, sorting and favorites remain available in the catalog.
